# Eugenia homework authoring rules

- Every new listening section must link to one specific, lesson-aligned activity. Do not send the learner to a general collection page or ask them to choose an activity.
- Every writing option must include an editable text box whose draft is saved locally in the learner's browser.
- Keep links and writing prompts aligned with the verified lesson scope. Do not add next-lesson preview material unless it is explicitly assigned.
- Keep learner-facing content in English and exclude private lesson-source data from all public files.
- Every exercise type—vocabulary, reading, listening, matching, gap-fill, dialogue, simulation and assessment—must use a natural, useful daily-life, study or work situation and have one defensible answer.
- In new exercises, put grammar inside plausible daily-life, study or work situations. Do not ask learners to name, explain or identify a grammar rule in a prompt.
- Every grammar-related question must have one defensible answer and include a short explanation shown after a correct response. Name the rule only in that feedback when it helps.
- Replace abstract true/false grammar statements with a contextual sentence, message, dialogue or practical choice.
