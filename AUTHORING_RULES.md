# Eugenia homework authoring rules

- Every new listening section must link to one specific, lesson-aligned activity. Do not send the learner to a general collection page or ask them to choose an activity.
- Every writing option must include an editable text box whose draft is saved locally in the learner's browser.
- Keep links and writing prompts aligned with the verified lesson scope. Do not add next-lesson preview material unless it is explicitly assigned.
- Keep learner-facing content in English and exclude private lesson-source data from all public files.
